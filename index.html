FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Most answers are here — no need to call us!

What time will my water be delivered?
Delivery happens between 8 AM and 11 AM depending on your area in Prayagraj. You can check your exact estimated time on the tracking section above.
What if I miss the delivery?
Our delivery partner will leave the can at your door or with your watchman. Missed deliveries are rescheduled same day at no extra charge.
How is the water purified?
We use a 7-stage RO + UV + TDS control purification process at our own plant in Prayagraj. TDS is maintained between 100–150 ppm — ideal for drinking.
Can I pause delivery for a few days?
Yes! WhatsApp us at least 1 day in advance and we'll pause your delivery. You won't be charged for paused days.
How do I pay?
We accept UPI, bank transfer, or cash. Monthly auto-debit via UPI is available. You get a digital receipt every month.
Is there a contract or lock-in period?
No contract at all. Cancel anytime with 3 days notice. We're confident you'll stay because of the quality!
